Name: My Lawn Care
Description: My Lawn Care is a web app where homeowners enter their ZIP code, grass type, and a description (or image) of their current yard and the yard they want, and get back a simple week-by-week plan for when and how to maintain your yard.

First Working Slice Allows Users to-

Start on the welcome screen and click “Let’s get started.”
Fill in the form: ZIP code, grass type (or “Not sure”), a short description of their yard now, and a description of the yard they want. Uploading photos is optional.
Get a plan by clicking “Make my plan.” The app shows a 4–6 week plan that says when and how high to mow, how much to water, and when to fertilize, plus extra steps for problems they mentioned, like bare spots or weeds.
Start over to try different inputs.

If a required field is left blank, the app shows a message explaining what’s missing instead of making a plan.
