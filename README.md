Name: My Lawn Care
Description: My Lawn Care is a web app where homeowners enter their ZIP code, grass type, and a description (or image) of their current yard and the yard they want, and get back a simple week-by-week plan for when and how to maintain your yard.

First Working Slice Allows Users to-

Start on the welcome screen and click “Let’s get started.”
Fill in the form: ZIP code, grass type (or “Not sure”), a short description of their yard now, and a description of the yard they want. Uploading photos is optional.
Get a plan by clicking “Make my plan.” The app shows a 4–6 week plan that says when and how high to mow, how much to water, and when to fertilize, plus extra steps for problems they mentioned, like bare spots or weeds.
Start over to try different inputs.

If a required field is left blank, the app shows a message explaining what’s missing instead of making a plan.

The plan comes from prewritten lawn-care tips, not a live AI or real weather, and the results page says so. Therefor, the AI behavior is simulated.

Current Limitations:
The AI is fake for now. The plan comes from prewritten lawn-care tips, not a real AI, and it doesn't check your actual weather. I plan to implement a real AI along with live weather scanning so the plan changes based on the current and upcoming weather.
It sometimes misreads what you type. The app looks for certain words, so it can get confused. For example, it gave shade tips when no one mentioned shade. I plan to get the AI to understand what the user means, or add simple checkboxes for common yard problems.
Photos don't do anything yet, and nothing is saved. You can upload yard photos, but the app only displays them and doesn't use them, and everything you type is lost when you close the page. I plan to have the AI look at the photos and let users save their yard info.
